# Cyber & Information Operations Intelligence — Phase 4

Phase 4 adds the Narrative & Information Operations Engine.

## Implemented capabilities

- Information-environment observation normalization
- Deterministic lexical similarity
- Narrative clustering
- Narrative velocity and reach scoring
- Propagation assessment
- Narrative mutation and evolution tracking
- Coordination indicators
- Information campaign assessment
- Explicit confidence, evidence status, caveats, and analytic judgments

## Analytical safeguards

The engine does not equate repetition with falsity, coordination with common control, or similarity with attribution. Coordination is an inferred assessment based on observable indicators such as temporal synchrony, textual similarity, source diversity, and account reuse. Actor attribution is not produced by this phase.

Manipulation-likelihood scoring is an analytic prioritization measure derived from propagation, coordination indicators, and strategic relevance. It is not a factual determination that content is false.

## Phase boundary

Phase 4 does not implement cyber-information hybrid fusion, geopolitical event correlation, escalation forecasting, or probability models. Those remain later phases.
